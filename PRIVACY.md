# Triple Clock privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy covers Triple Clock for Android (no.erik.tripleclock), published by erikhardlyworking, including its clock, cat and plant widgets.

Contact: workinghardlyforyou@gmail.com
Public policy: https://github.com/erikhardlyworking/tripleclock/blob/main/PRIVACY.md

Information used by the app

Triple Clock works offline. The app does not collect or send your personal data to the developer or third parties. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics SDKs or Internet permission. All included artwork is bundled with the app.

The app stores your settings locally on your device. These can include selected timezones, custom clock and event labels, countdown deadlines, language and calendar choices, widget appearance and a selected app to open when a widget is tapped. Device time, timezone, language and display settings are used locally to render the widgets. Following your phone's timezone does not access your location or require location permission.

If you choose an app to open on tap, Triple Clock lists launchable apps on your device and stores the package identifier of your selection locally. That list and selection are not sent to the developer. The app you choose opens only through your action and has its own privacy practices.

Storage, deletion and security

Settings are kept in Android's app-private storage. Triple Clock does not operate a server or cloud account, and disables Android backup and device transfer for its private settings. Removing a widget removes its widget-specific settings. You can remove all locally stored Triple Clock settings by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app. Android and your launcher control their own system data.

Support and external services

If you email support, the developer receives the email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. This information is used to respond to your request and retained only as needed to handle that correspondence. You may request deletion by emailing the same address. Do not include sensitive information unless it is necessary for your request.

Opening the public policy uses your browser to visit GitHub. GitHub, your email provider, Google Play and Android may process information under their own privacy policies when you use those services. Such processing is separate from the offline operation of Triple Clock.

Children

Triple Clock does not collect personal information through the app, including from children.

Policy updates

Changes to this policy will be posted at the public policy address with an updated date. For privacy questions or requests, contact workinghardlyforyou@gmail.com.
